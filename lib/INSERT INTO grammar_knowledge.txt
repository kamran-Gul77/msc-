(title, rule, explanation, examples, proficiency_level)
VALUES
-- BEGINNER LEVEL
('Past Simple',
 'Use the past simple tense for completed actions in the past.',
 'The past simple tense describes actions that happened at a specific time in the past.',
 'I visited my grandmother yesterday. He watched a movie last night.',
 'beginner'),

('Future Simple',
 'Use "will" + verb for actions in the future.',
 'The future simple tense indicates actions that will happen later.',
 'I will call you tomorrow. She will travel to Paris next week.',
 'beginner'),

('Countable & Uncountable Nouns',
 'Use "many" with countable nouns and "much" with uncountable nouns.',
 'Countable nouns can be counted, uncountable nouns cannot.',
 'I have many apples. There is much water in the bottle.',
 'beginner'),

('Simple Prepositions',
 'Use "in", "on", "at" for time and place.',
 'Prepositions indicate relationships of time, place, and movement.',
 'I am at the park. The book is on the table. We met in July.',
 'beginner'),

-- INTERMEDIATE LEVEL
('Present Perfect',
 'Use present perfect to link past actions with the present.',
 'This tense shows actions that happened at an unspecified time in the past.',
 'I have finished my homework. She has visited London twice.',
 'intermediate'),

('Past Continuous',
 'Use past continuous for ongoing actions in the past.',
 'Past continuous describes actions that were in progress at a specific time in the past.',
 'I was reading when he called. They were playing football all afternoon.',
 'intermediate'),

('Conditional Sentences Type 1',
 'Use "if + present simple, will + verb" for real future possibilities.',
 'Type 1 conditionals describe possible events in the future.',
 'If it rains, I will stay home. She will pass if she studies.',
 'intermediate'),

('Modals for Advice',
 'Use "should" and "ought to" for giving advice.',
 'These modals suggest actions that are recommended but not mandatory.',
 'You should see a doctor. He ought to apologize.',
 'intermediate'),

('Relative Clauses',
 'Use "who", "which", "that" to give extra information about nouns.',
 'Relative clauses describe or identify nouns more clearly.',
 'The man who called you is my uncle. The book that I bought is new.',
 'intermediate'),

-- ADVANCED LEVEL
('Passive Voice',
 'Use passive voice to focus on the action rather than the subject.',
 'The subject receives the action rather than performing it.',
 'The cake was baked by Mary. The report will be finished tomorrow.',
 'advanced'),

('Reported Speech',
 'Use reporting verbs and change tense when reporting someone’s words.',
 'Reported speech conveys what someone else said, with tense adjustments.',
 'He said he was tired. She told me she would come later.',
 'advanced'),

('Conditional Sentences Type 3',
 'Use "if + past perfect, would have + past participle" for unreal past situations.',
 'Type 3 conditionals describe hypothetical past events that didn’t happen.',
 'If I had studied, I would have passed. She would have come if she had been invited.',
 'advanced'),

('Gerunds and Infinitives',
 'Use gerunds (-ing) and infinitives (to + verb) correctly after certain verbs.',
 'Some verbs require gerunds, others infinitives.',
 'I enjoy swimming. I want to learn Spanish.',
 'advanced'),

('Advanced Phrasal Verbs',
 'Phrasal verbs combine verbs with particles to create new meanings.',
 'Many common English verbs form phrases that change the meaning significantly.',
 'She gave up smoking. He ran into an old friend yesterday.',
 'advanced');
